The style source is 张择端《清明上河图》, Palace Museum, Beijing — the Wikimedia Commons scan
(public domain). `full_500.jpg` is that scan at 500 px tall (1 px ≈ 0.5 mm), the `style_*.png`
windows are 930×500 cuts of it at viewing scale, the `detail_*.png` are 2.5× crops. The
38 414 px original (`full_original.jpg`, 18 MB) is not in the repo; fetch it from Commons if you
want to cut new windows.
