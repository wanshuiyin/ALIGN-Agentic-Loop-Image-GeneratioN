# mod-crowd.js · placement grammar

Files: `src/mod-crowd.js`, `src/scene-crowd.js` (self-check scene), `src/harness-crowd.html`, `review/module-crowd.png`. Contract: `wiki/interfaces.md` §2(g). Places, never draws; no `add()` anywhere. Every random value comes from `ctx.R/rr/ri`.

## Public functions

### `CROWD.place(ctx, spec) → [{x,y,h,pose,dir,z,tint,prop}, …]`

| field | meaning |
|---|---|
| `kind` | `queue` · `cluster` · `episode` · `stream` (default) · `ring` · `rail` · `stairs` · `deck` |
| `zone` | `{pts,w,z,dir,seats}` — `pts` `[[x,y],…]` is the band centre line (stream), the rail line (rail), the stair line bottom→top (stairs), or a queue's own path from the slot outward; `w` half-width of the band (px) — a stream is walked in `round(2w/0.55h)` depth lanes (1–4), stairs use it as lateral spread (default 0.25 h); `z` the deck's z when figures stand on a structure (bridge deck, boat) — with it the ground test is skipped and every figure gets that z; `dir` a direction vector: the way a queue extends from its slot (default `[1,0.08]`), or the "up" sense of a vertical stair; `seats` a boat's seat list for `deck`. A box `{x0,x1,y0,y1}` is accepted as a horizontal band (centre line at mid-y, `w` = half its height). |
| `focus` | `[x,y]` (or `{x,y}`). Queue: the slot the line ends at (counter, ferry gate) — the first person stands 0.45–0.6 h from it, everyone faces it. Cluster / ring / episode: the centre they form round (the incident). Stream / rail: what people look at — stream figures within 2.2 h of it turn to face it and 45 % of the walkers among them stop (`stand`, or `point` with `prop:'arm'`); rail figures face it (the plan's "all arms point at the same span"). |
| `n` | count. Defaults: queue `ri(4,9)` (+ the straggler), cluster `ri(3,6)`, episode `ri(3,7)` participants (the incident figure in `roles[0]` is extra), ring `ri(8,14)` dancers, rail `L/(1.1h)`, stairs `L/(0.75h)`, deck = all seats (a smaller `n` thins the seats at random), stream from `density`. |
| `shape` | **≠ added** episode only: `ring` (default) · `clump` · `knot` — see the kind. |
| `roles`, `spread` | episode only. `roles[0]` may be a figure spec `{pose,dir,prop,h,z,tint,x,y,hs}` — the incident itself (a `vendor`, a `squat` with `prop:'ebike'` for a fallen rider, a photographer's subject); it is placed at the focus (or its own `x,y`) without a clash test and returned first. The remaining entries name the participants' poses in order (crowd vocabulary), any missing one drawn from `mix` (default `cluster`). `spread` scales the distances (default 1). |
| `density` | stream only, figures per 100 px of band length (`n = density·L/100`, default 4). Plan values: 汉正街/户部巷 4–6, 江滩 2–3, 桥面 1–2, 东湖 0.5–1. |
| `mix` | pose weights `{walk:5, dan:1.2, …}` or a preset name: `street`, `quay`, `bridge`, `park` (streams); each other kind has its own default (`CROWD.MIX`). In a stream 20 % of figures are drawn from the carrying poses (`dan porter basket bag cart pushbike ebike veg`) and the rest from the others, whatever the weights. |
| `watchers`, `watchMix`, `r` | ring only: number of onlookers (default `ri(4,7)`), their mix (default `watch`), the ring radius (default `h·(0.13n+0.1)`, which puts dancers ≈ 0.62 h apart). |

Output spec: `x,y` ground contact (bottom-centre); `h = 60·ctx.depth(y)` (children 0.5 h); `pose` from the vocabulary below; `dir` +1 facing right / −1 facing left; `z = y`, or `zone.z` when given; `tint` `none | indigo | zhusha` (36 % / 6 %, at most one 朱砂 per group, two above 12); `prop` `'' | dan umbrella phone bowl bike ebike cart sack basket bag stick camera backpack pole oar broom flag arm hand`.

Pose vocabulary emitted (the figure module's `POSES` must carry these names, or main.js maps them): `walk stand queue phone talk point eat squat sit dan porter basket cart pushbike ebike umbrella stick photo tourist run sweep veg vendor pole row child climb rail dance`. New this round: `talk` and `rail` may carry `prop:'arm'` (a gesture toward the neighbour — main.js's `toFig` should let that pick `pointing` for `talk` and pass the arm to `leanRail` if the figure module grows one); `point`/`phone` in a stream may face against the flow — keep their `dir`, do not override it with `opt.lookX`/`pointDir` unless the group has a focus. `bag` and `flag` in a mix become `walk` with that prop; `child` in a mix becomes an adult (`walk`, or `climb` on stairs) with `prop:'hand'` plus a separate half-height `child` spec beside it — children are their own specs, not a composite pose.

Kind by kind:
- **queue** — a snaking path from the slot outward (`zone.pts`, or `zone.dir` with a lateral random walk of ±0.28 h every 1.3 h), people at 0.45–0.6 h steps with a ±0.07 h lateral shuffle, 30 % `phone`, the rest `queue`/`stand`; from the third person on, one in six turns back to `talk` to the next; 10 % have a child. A person whose feet would land in a stamped footprint moves 0.22 h further back (three tries). **≠ added** a straggler 1.5 h behind the last, `walk` toward the slot — the line has an end that is still arriving.
- **episode** **≠ rewritten (D-08, B2 tell 3)** — an incident that *propagates*. `spec.shape` picks the silhouette (`ring` · `clump` · `knot`; read from `roles[0].pose` when absent: `photo` → clump, `vendor`/`veg` → knot, else ring). The stream's sense is `zone.dir` if given, else *against* the incident's `dir` (a wrong-way rider). Reaction poses `recoil block yield look` are emitted when `FIGURE.POSES` has them, else `point`(+`arm`) / `point` / `stand` / `phone`.
  - **ring** (the e-bike): ring 1 inside 1.2 h on the side the incident heads for — 3–4 in `recoil / block / yield / recoil`, facing it, x within the incident's own width or just past it and feet staggered ±0.2 h in depth so the bodies overlap it and each other (the last may be behind); a **consequential gap** on the far side, 1.2 h → 2.7–3.7 h, nobody in it; ring 2 at 1.2–2.5 h — 1–2 halted on the approach side facing in (`roles` name them), 1–2 `look` figures just beyond the gap's far end with their feet still the stream's way (head turned back).
  - **clump** (the photographers): low and wide — a row along x at 0.35–0.55 h, `squat`(camera) · `rail`(camera, feet 0.5 h below the rail line, **z = zone.z − 3** so the stamped rail hides the legs) · `photo`, heights ×0.9–1, y jitter ±0.08 h, **all facing the incident's `dir`**.
  - **knot** (the vendor): tight and vertical — 3–4 customers stacked in depth right in front of the vendor (x within 0.25–0.55 h, feet from −0.5 h behind to +0.35 h in front, overlapping), then a **queue tail** of the remaining n at 0.45–0.6 h steps leading off along the stream (`queue`/`phone`, facing the vendor); a small gap of 0.4–1.5 h behind the vendor.
  - Every episode writes `ctx.reg.gaps` (boxes) and `ctx.reg.knots` (`{x,y,h}`); a **stream placed afterwards** puts nobody in a gap (the clump walks past it) and compresses its spacing ×0.6 within 4 h of a knot, both inside clumps and between them. Place the episodes first, the streams after.
  - Overlap inside an episode: up to 60 % when the *feet* differ by ≥ 0.06 h — the clash test now uses the feet's y as depth (`box.d`) everywhere, so figures sharing a deck `z` can still stand in front of one another. On a deck each figure's `z` is `zone.z ± 1.5` by its feet (nearer feet, higher z) so the front body's lines win where they cross; stays under the rail's z.
- **cluster** — 3–6 on a flattened ring (y-radius 0.42 × x-radius) at 0.8–1.5 h from the focus, angular gaps drawn 0.5–1.5 × even and an opening of 0–28 % of the circle, all facing the focus (random when directly in front of/behind it).
- **stream** — **≠ rewritten (D-07 pulse)** the band is split into depth lanes; each lane is walked from a random start in *clumps*: 2–4 people (15 % of clumps a single) at 0.4–0.7 h along, then a gap of 1.5–3 h before the next clump. Inside a clump, each member is: 25 % the front of a *pair stopped face to face* (`talk` → `talk`/`stand` 0.36 h on, offset 0.06 h in depth so sleeves cross; half get `prop:'arm'` — the bargain), 15 % *stopped and turned against the flow* (`phone`, or `point` facing back the way it came), else a walker from the mix (20 % carrying). Direction runs: 68 % of clumps keep the previous clump's direction. Lateral position drifts as a bounded random walk (±0.2 h per step) so clump members sit at different depths and may overlap up to 40 % when |Δz| ≥ 4. Gaps between clumps are scaled 0.45–2.2× so the lane's share of `n` fits its length (the grammar's natural cost is ≈ 1.12 h per person); inside-clump spacing only tightens, to 0.7×, never widens. `density` keeps its meaning — people per 100 px on average; the walk yields 92–97 % of `n` and the remainder is attached beside existing clumps (0.45–0.9 h along), never dropped into the gaps. `focus` behaviour unchanged.
- **ring** — dancers on the flattened ring with gaps 0.7–1.3 × even and radius ±7 %, facing the centre; watchers in 1–3 clumps at 1.35–1.7 r, 70 % on the near side, each clump within ±0.45 h, facing the ring.
- **rail** — **≠ rewritten** the same pulse as the stream: clumps of 2–4 (15 % singles) at 0.4–0.7 h along the rail, gaps of 1.5–3 h between clumps, gap scale 0.5–2.5× so `n` fits; short of `n`, extras join an existing clump 0.4–0.7 h beside a member. Within a clump, 30 % of the people after the first are *turned back to the one just placed* (dir against the rail's sense; half with `prop:'arm'`) — talking to a neighbour instead of looking at the view. Heights ×0.92–1.08. Feet at `rail y + 0.46–0.54 h` so the rail crosses at the waist — pose `rail` draws waist-up; **≠ changed** `z` is `zone.z − 3` (the integrator stamps the rail in front of the deck figures; the rail hides the legs), else the rail line's y. `point` in the mix becomes `rail` with `prop:'arm'`; `focus` still turns everyone toward it (the turned-back talkers excepted).
- **stairs** — climbers along the line at 0.5–0.9 h steps with the 20 % lull, lateral ±w; 70 % ascend (dir = the line's x-sense, or `zone.dir`), 25 % are pairs 0.35 h apart on opposite sides of the line; `stand`/`point` climbers face a random way (they are looking at the view).
- **deck** — `zone.seats` `[{x,y,pose,dir,h}]` passed through, `pose` default `sit`, `z` = `zone.z` or the seat's y.

### `CROWD.footprint(ctx, fig) → {x0,x1,y0,y1,z,inside}`

The box the placement rules used, ready for `ctx.masks.stampZ`. Width 0.35 h, or 0.6 h with a wide prop (`dan umbrella bike ebike cart`); height h, or 0.62 h `sit`, 0.55 h `squat`/`veg`, 0.7 h `vendor`; pose `rail` spans `y−h … y−0.45h` only. Inside the box the silhouette is: head — half-width 0.2 w for the top 0.14 h; body — half-width widening from 0.34 w to 0.57 w at the feet (wide props: 0.5 w throughout). This is the same rule the figure module is contracted to (0.35 h / 0.6 h), so either module's footprint may be stamped.

### `CROWD.spreadAlong(ctx, pts, n, jitter) → [[x,y,tx,ty,s], …]`

`n` points along the polyline by arc length, each moved by up to ±`jitter`/2 of a spacing (jitter 1 = points may nearly touch, never cross); `tx,ty` the unit tangent there, `s` the arc length. Takes `ctx` because the jitter uses `ctx.rr`.

## Rules as implemented

- Feet never inside `masks.solidAt`: tested at the feet, at ±0.35 w beside them and 3 px up, for every kind that stands on the ground (not `rail`/`deck`, and not when `zone.z` is given).
- Footprint boxes: overlap ≤ 4 % of the smaller box is ignored (touching); above that, it is allowed only when |Δz| ≥ 6 and it stays ≤ 40 %. Applied within clusters, rings and stairs, and always against figures from earlier `place` calls. **≠ changed** within a stream the depth threshold is 4 (clump members overlap), within an episode 4 and 50 % — `clash(b,o,dz,maxOv)` takes the group's thresholds. Queue, rail and deck members are exempt from the within-group test (a queue and a rail row overlap by nature). A clashing candidate is nudged up to three times by 0.12–0.35 h (queue and rail: moved along their line instead), then dropped.
- **≠ added** `ctx.reg.placed`: every placed figure's box goes there so later groups keep off earlier ones. Consequence for main.js: place the events first (queues, clusters, rings, rail rows, stairs), then the streams around them, and stamp figure footprints only after **all** `place` calls of a station — a stamped figure box would otherwise fail the feet test for anyone standing behind it.
- x is kept ≥ `ctx.CW+3`, y within 10 … `SH−2`.

## Round 3 (B2 tell 3, D-08)

`review/module-crowd.png`: the three bridge silhouettes in a row on the one street band, right to left — the e-bike ring (`ebike` incident facing right, the stream running left), the photographers' clump at a stamped kerb, the vendor's knot with its queue tail — and a 52-figure `street` stream placed after them; gap boxes drawn in faint 朱砂 for the check only.

What the render showed: at 1× the band reads as knot – empty run – knot – knot with a tail, not as an even stream. The e-bike is buried in four overlapping bodies with arms out, the gap to its left is ~2 h of bare band with two turned-back figures at its far edge, and the stream on the e-bike's far side packs to about half its usual spacing. The clump sits lower than the stream (kneelers and waist-up rail leaners on the kerb line), the knot is three heads stacked in depth and three singles trailing off. The stubs cannot show recoil, kneeling or a turned head — those are the figure module's; only positions, facing, overlaps and the gap are on show here.

**main.js must call differently.** (1) `PMAP` gains `recoil:'recoil', yield:'yield', block:'block', look:'look'` — the module only emits them when `FIGURE.POSES` has them, otherwise the older names arrive. (2) The bridge: the e-bike episode as `shape:'ring'` (or just `roles:[{pose:'ebike',…}]`) with `zone:{z:NZ,dir:[-1,0]}` so the gap opens on the correct side of the deck, `n:7–8`; the photographers `roles:[{pose:'photo',dir:…}]` at the rail line's y (feet of the `rail` members land 0.5 h below it); the vendor `roles:[{pose:'vendor',…},'eat','stand','eat']`, `n:6–7` for a tail of 2–3. (3) Order on a station: episodes → rail → streams (the streams read `ctx.reg.gaps/knots`, which only exist after the episodes). (4) Deck figures now come back with `z = zone.z ± 1.5` and rail leaners with `z = zone.z − 3`; `toFig` should pass `z` through unchanged. (5) The `look` figure's `dir` is its walking direction — the head turns *against* `dir`; do not overwrite it with `lookX`/`pointDir`.
