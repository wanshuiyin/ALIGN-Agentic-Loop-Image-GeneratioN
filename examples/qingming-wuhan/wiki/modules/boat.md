## Public surface
```
BOAT.footprint(ctx,spec) → {x0,y0,x1,y1,z,inside,edge,parts}   pure; geometry cached on spec.bt — pass the same spec object to build()
BOAT.build(ctx,spec)     → rec, pushed to ctx.reg.boats
rec = {fp, seats:[{x,y,pose,dir,z,h}], type,x,y,len,dir,z,moving,bowX,sternX, spec:{dir,moving,len,type}, slots:{deck:deckY(x)}}
```
`spec = {x,y,type,len,dir,z,moving,mastFolded,clearance?,ropeTo?,tow?,towTo?}` (`rec` also carries `clearance`): `(x,y)` = waterline at the **right** end, `len` extends left, `dir=+1` bow at the right, `z` default `y`. `rec.spec` is the shape mod-water reads (`hull(b)` uses `b.fp` + `b.spec.dir/moving/len`).

## Deviations / decisions
- **People on boats are 36·depth px**, not 60: the plan's boat lengths make a boat only five shore-figures long, and the original's boats are 6–10 heads long. Superstructure heights are tied to that figure height (`Math.max(k·fb, k'·fh)`), so decks and cabins clear the heads.
- Occlusion polygon = hull + every deck/cabin box + the mast strip; the stays and the 橹 are not in the footprint (water may cross them).
- 朱砂 waterline bands and the 海事 stripe go in `ST.INDIGO` with the other 朱砂 (`redLine` → `ctx.rline` pieces with the hull's z, so the band stops behind figures and trees that stand in front of the hull — the integrator found it running across them when drawn as one unclipped `ctx.add` stroke). Since round 3 the band is never one line (see below).
- Line grades (D-06 hierarchy): sheer, stem, rounded stern, waterline and the mat barrel's arc are primary `bline` 0.75–0.9 / 205–225; the bilge line, plank seams, deck edges, rails and window openings are structural 0.55–0.7 / 140–180; mat weave, tarp folds, stays and shrouds are texture 0.4–0.55 / 80–130 via an 8-mark batcher that drops marks hidden at `z`.

## Round 2 (B1 tell 7: "rectangular windowed boxes with straight coloured stripes")
- **Hull**: sheer sag ×1.6; every hull carries a bilge line 30 % down the freeboard and plank/plating seams as smooth curves that follow the sheer near the top and flatten toward the waterline, converging on the bow; the 赭石 wash is heavier and the bilge darkens with 淡墨 toward the water. ferry/tug/louchuan/haishi take `roundStern` inside `hull()`: the stern is a 3-point curve bulging aft of the stern post down to the waterline (`b.sternCurve`), the waterline and 朱砂 band start at `b.wlS`. song/barge/huazi/duchuan keep the squared 汴河 stern.
- **Ferry / tug superstructure**: window rows are openings (4×3 rects every 6 px, a sill line, wall between) instead of full-height mullions; each deck edge throws a 1-px 淡墨 shadow; the funnel has a cap ring and a band; davits with a lifeboat hang under the upper deck on the side away from the bikes. The wheelhouse and funnel were shorter than the 36-px passengers standing in front of them (invisible in v1) — now `max(fb, 0.95·fh)` / `max(1.2·fb, 0.8·fh)`, and the ferry's passengers keep to the top deck aft of the wheelhouse at uneven spacing.
- **Mat cabin**: the barrel's arc is primary, an inner arc marks the mat's bound edge, a dark ridge cane runs along the crown, and the weave (every 1.6 px, stopping short of the ridge) alternates 8-px bands of lean with the heavier band at alpha 105–130 so the plaiting reads as a barrel at 1×.
- **楼船**: the roof is a hipped canopy on three posts standing `1.12·fh` above the open top deck, with a balustrade; the top-deck seats stand on the deck under it (the v1 seats stood on the roof). Deck edges get the 淡墨 shadow.
- **Tackle**: every `song` has bow/stern stays, a pair of shrouds to the gunwale, a slack halyard to the mast foot, a coiled rope on deck, and — when `spec.ropeTo=[x,y]` is given — a 纤绳 from the mast to that point (main.js: the 纤夫 or bollard on the bank). Tugs have a rope coil aft; with `spec.tow=true, spec.towTo=[x,y]` a slack catenary runs from the stern bitts to that point; if `towTo` lies ahead of the bow the tug is a pusher and a tight lashing runs from the bow instead. Tug tyres now hang at the waterline.
- **朱砂 band** (`redLine`): drawn as runs — it stops under each hanging tyre (`b.fenderX`) and lifts once or twice mid-run as the brush reloads; still `rline` at the hull's z, so figures and trees in front cut it as before.
- Helpers added: `rope(ctx,z,a,b,sagK,w,al)` (catenary `bline`), `coil(ctx,b,x,y,r)` (flattened spiral). Public surface, seats, rec shape, stages unchanged.

## Round 3 (B2 tell 5 boat half + tell 7 「vermilion stripe mechanically even」; D-08 grades)
- **Grades**: every weight and alpha in the file was written against v2's primary 0.8/210; `wk(ctx)`/`ak(ctx,a)` scale them to whatever `ctx.INK.primary` is now, so the hull and rigging thin with core (D-08 ≈ 25 %) without touching each call. Texture marks (weave, folds, stays) go through the mark batcher as plain polylines and never had heads.
- **The hull sits in the water**: the waterline is no longer a drawn primary edge — it is a structural line broken into 2–3 runs with gaps, bellied by the displacement, and at the stem and the stern post a short curl of meniscus climbs the hull above the line and falls away. The lower third of the freeboard takes a 淡墨 band in the 花青 pass (`ST.INDIGO`), laid twice toward the waterline so the turn of the bilge darkens into the water. The old 赭石-stage bilge 淡墨 is replaced by this.
- **Barge**: `b.mounds` are still 3–5 spans, now of unequal width (weights 0.7–1.4) and height, but the tarp is one silhouette (`b.tarp`, a `bline` sampled every 3 px from the max of overlapping raised-cosine humps, ×1.5 wider than their spans so the valleys stay above the deck, tapering to the deck at both ends). Fold lines lean away from each crest toward the valleys at texture grade; ties (`b.valleys` + both ends) go over the low point and down past the gunwale to the hull side; a hem line runs near the deck. One footprint part for the whole load (`b.parts[1]`), cabin still last.
- **Ferry / tug**: the lower deck house is a wall band with 6–8 openings (`openings()`: pitch/0.42 wide, wall wider than glass, each holding a 淡墨 interior stroke, a sill under); the upper cabin 5–7; tug storeys 3–5 each, wheelhouse 3–4 tall panes. Upper-deck rail is one line with posts at 12 px (was 8). The wheelhouse and funnel (ferry) and the top storey (tug) take 淡墨 on the aft 30–40 % of their face in `ST.INDIGO` so they read as boxes with a lit and a shaded side.
- **朱砂 band** (`redLine`): 3–5 ruled pieces of unequal length (weights 0.6–1.6), each with its own alpha `ri(60,130)` and width ×0.8–1.15, gaps of 3–8 px; still cut under the tug's tyres and clipped at the hull's z. The 海事 stripe uses the same routine. No band on any boat is continuous.
- **Mat cabin**: the weave bands are of unequal width (5–11 px, edges drawn once per boat at build time) and the two lengthwise canes sit at heights drawn per boat (0.28–0.45 and 0.58–0.82 of the arc).
- Public surface, seats, `rec` shape, stages, footprint union unchanged; main.js calls nothing differently. `harness-boat.html` carries the D-08 core re-embedded.

## Round 4 (B3 tell 4 「舟船还缺"包起来的体积"」; D-10 (3))
- **New primitive — the volume comes first, the detail sits on it.** Two constructions beside the old ones (`obl`, `barrelGeom`/`cabinPoly`, `houseGeom`/`drawHouse`/`sideWash`), used inside the existing types; public surface, `spec`, seats, `rec` shape and stages unchanged, main.js calls nothing differently. The boat's oblique: a point deeper into the boat rises by the depth and shifts toward the stern (`ctx.OBL[0]` scaled by `dir`, as the arch module's boxes do), so every cabin and deck house shows three faces — the near side, the top turning away, and its **aft** end face.
- **Mat 舱棚 = a barrel lying along the boat** (`barrelGeom`): `S(x,u)` is the point on the surface at length `x` and turning angle `u` (near eave → crown → far eave); the section is steep from the eaves with a broad, nearly flat crown (a mat 篷, not a drum), so a band of the top shows between the ridge cane on the near slope and the silhouette where the far slope turns out of sight. The aft end is closed by the complete arch — the end face — holding the doorway (two jambs on the face's foot line, a low arch between) and 淡墨 on its far half, darker in the doorway; the forward end is the near arc up to the turning angle only. The weave is laid ON the surface: every strand an arc `S(x,u)` bending with the barrel, the pitch tightening toward the ends, stopping at the end face, and every other strand continuing over the crown into the top band fainter. The canes are lines of constant `u`. The crown bows along the length; the ends keep most of the height. The near foot stands a strip of walkway in from the gunwale; the deck plank lines stop at the cabin. Grades: silhouette, forward arc, aft arch and the aft wall corner primary; ridge cane, eave, far wall edge, foot of the end face, doorway structural; weave and canes texture. 花青 on the near slope only (the top band stays pale), 赭石 on the wall. The cabin's 复勾 (D-10 a) is one heaviest-grade stroke along the eave under the mat over the middle of its length, in `ST.FINISH`.
- **Deck houses = boxes** (`houseGeom`/`drawHouse`): `h={x0,x1,top,y1?}` — a house on the hull stands on the far deck line, a house on a deck above has a flat foot `y1`. Side; top surface as a parallelogram of the boat's depth slanted at both ends, its far edge drawn only fore and aft of the house standing on it (`cover=[x0,x1]`); aft end face with 淡墨 0.15. The aft corner where side meets end face is one primary line, the side's top edge primary, the rest structural; windows stay on the side, clear of the corners (unchanged callers). 花青 goes on the side face only (`sideWash`). Applied to the ferry's three houses (the aft-30 % shade is gone — the end face is the shade), the tug's three storeys, the 楼船's three decks (cover = the deck/roof above), the barge cabin and the 汴河 stern house.
- **Hull**: the sheer strake — the band between the gunwale and the 护舷 line — is left blank silk (no 赭石, no 淡墨): the lit top of the hull's turn; the 淡墨 under the gunwale now begins below the strake and is lighter, so the side rounds from light at the top to the dark bilge into the water.

## Round 5 (B4 tells 3 + 6, D-12 (2), (5), (a), (b))
- **Tug under a beam — `spec.clearance`** (D-12 (2)): the height above the waterline that nothing may exceed. Storeys whose foot is already above the line are omitted; the last one kept is cut down so its roof sits 3.5 px under the line; the signal mast is not folded but LOWERED — it lies aft along the deckhouse roof out of its tabernacle (two cheeks), the yard beside it on the near side of the roof, the far end on a crutch standing on the aft deck with a lashing, the halyard coiled at the foot, a slack rope from the mast end to the winch; the funnel is hinged down to a short stub with its cap ring lying on the roof beside it. The footprint shrinks to hull + kept storeys + the flat mast strip; seats' `h` is capped at the room under the line, and `rec.clearance` carries the value so main.js can cap its own extra winch hands the same way (`h ≤ deck − (y − clearance) − 1`). `mastFolded` is ignored when `clearance` is set. Nothing else on the tug changed (the 45° fold stays for tugs without a clearance).
- **Mat 舱棚 as an occluding shell** (D-12 (5)): `barrelGeom` now also returns the three face polygons — near face (wall + slope up to the ridge cane at u=0.35), top band (0.35 → U), end face (the aft arch closed by its foot); `GEO.song` keeps them as `b.shell`, and `build()` stamps them at **z+2** (`stampShell`) before drawing, so the far gunwale, the deck planks, the stays running aft and anything standing behind the cabin are cut by the shell; the cabin's own strokes go at z+1. The weave is clipped to the near slope only: transverse arcs `S(x,u)` for u ≤ 0.34, stopping at the ridge cane and at the end face; within each band (5–11 px, lean alternating, every other heavier) the strands fan so the pitch is ×0.7 at the eave and ×1 at the crown; the strands that ran over the crown are gone — the top band carries 3–4 faint lengthwise canes (alpha 45–65) and silk between. Colour by face (D-12 b): 花青 on the near slope only, the top band silk, the end face 淡墨 only (0.10 over the whole face, 0.15 on its far half, 0.30 in the doorway), 赭石 on the wall. The eave under the mat stays the one heaviest stroke (FINISH).
- **Hull thickness** (D-12 a): the two broad lower-third 淡墨 washes are replaced by one narrow continuous band of 淡墨 (INDIGO, ink 0.22, `pool` dabs r 1.3 step 1.2) just above the waterline — 2 px at bow and stern, 4 px amidships (sin^0.8 of the length) — the turn of the bilge into the water; the sheer strake stays blank, the 赭石 and the light 淡墨 under the strake unchanged. Applies to every hull type through `drawHull`.

## Round 6 (B5 tell 5 — 篷船仍薄, primitive, minimal; song only)
- **The mat 篷 is one round shell** (`barrelGeom`): the section is a true semi-ellipse — turning angle u → θ = πu, height `ry·sin θ`, depth `(1−cos θ)/2` of the barrel's width — so the near face rises steeply from the eave, rounds continuously through the crown at u = 0.5 and turns down out of sight past U (computed from ry/dd, ≈0.55, where the projected height peaks). No wall+slope any more: the wall is a 0.2·h strake (`cab.wall`), the ridge cane is gone, the three shell polygons are near (foot → crown), top band (crown → U), end face (the full arch) — same `b.shell` shape, `stampShell` unchanged. The oblique depth is 1.45·dw (was 1.2) so the end face has room for a mouth.
- **Lines**: eave, forward arc, aft arch, silhouette, wall corners as before; on the mat itself only one faint lengthwise cane near the crown (u 0.44, alpha 50–65). The 3–4 top canes, the two binding canes and the ridge cane are removed — they were the "long horizontals flattening the slope".
- **Weave, sparse**: one transverse arc every 3–4 px, following `S(x,u)`; each in two pieces — eave to u 0.3 at 0.45/95–125, then u 0.3–0.45 at 0.38/45–70 and only 70 % of them — so the plaiting fades into the lit crown. Bands of 5–11 px lean alternately ±0.6–1.1 px.
- **篷口**: the whole arch of the aft end face is 淡墨 0.34 (`pool` dabs step 1.1), its far half a further 0.2, and the doorway's lintel is the only line across it; jambs and jamb arch removed. 花青 gathers on the lower 86 % of the near face's height; the crown and top band stay silk.
- Seen: at 1× both 汴河 cabins read as a rounded roll with a dark aft mouth; at 6× the near face turns without a ridge, the weave is a sparse vertical rhythm bending into the crown, no lengthwise line crosses the slope. Other types untouched; main.js calls unchanged.
