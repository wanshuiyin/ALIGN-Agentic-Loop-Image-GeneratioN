# mod-tree.js · 树石

Files: `src/mod-tree.js` (module), `src/scene-tree.js` (self-check scene: one of each kind on a bank, a two-peak hill with a valley, the 東湖 grove with its central tree featured **and** woven and the 槐 beside it woven, a woven 梧桐 at the grove's end, a lone 杂 on a far bank with knot and weave together), `src/harness-tree.html` (harness pointed at both), `review/module-tree.png` (the render). Contract: `wiki/interfaces.md` §2(d), §3. Global `TREE = {footprint, build, bank, hill, hillFootprint}` (unchanged in rounds 4–11: the growth order lives inside the same calls). ES5, no DOM, no `Math.random`; all randomness is spent while the skeleton is grown and while marks are queued, never inside a stroke closure.

## Public functions

### `TREE.footprint(ctx, spec)` → `{x0,x1,y0,y1,z,inside(x,y)}`
Pure (no `add`). Grows the tree once and caches it as `spec.sk`, so `build()` later draws exactly the tree the mask was stamped with. **Pass the same spec object to both calls**; a copy would grow a different tree.

### `TREE.build(ctx, spec)` → `{fp, slots:{crown:[x,y], top:[x,y], kind}}`
Queues every mark, pushes the record into `ctx.reg.trees`, returns it. `crown` is the fork (where the trunk ends), `top` the highest point drawn — useful for placing birds, kites or a sign that must clear the tree.

### `TREE.bank(ctx, spec)` → `{poly}`
Draws a 坡岸 from an edge polyline. Ground: no footprint, nothing stamped. Every mark checks `masks.hidden` at its own y (or `spec.z`), so call it after phase A; figures and trunks standing on the bank then cut the 皴 and the wash under them. Returns the wash polygon (edge + foot) in case the integrator wants to stamp water up to it.

### `TREE.hillFootprint(ctx, spec)` → `{x0,x1,y0,y1,z,inside(x,y)}` · `TREE.hill(ctx, spec)` → `{fp, slots:{ridge, top, kind:'hill'}}` (round 2)
A hill body for 蛇山 / 龟山 / 磨山. Spec `{pts, depth, z, tone}`: `pts` the ridge polyline **right to left**, `depth` the visible body height in px under the ridge (default 40), `z` the mask depth (default: ridge top y − 1, so anything standing on the body — buildings, pines — is nearer and its footprint cuts the hill's marks), `tone` `'ink'` (淡墨, default) or `'huaqing'` (淡花青). The ridge is resampled and cached as `spec.hk`, so pass the same object to both calls. Footprint = the polygon between the ridge and the foot curve `hk.footY` (round 6: a foot that lies flat, not the ridge shifted down). `hill()` pushes `{kind:'hill', fp, slots}` into `ctx.reg.zones`. **main.js**: `ctx.masks.stampZ(TREE.hillFootprint(ctx,h))` in phase A before the buildings and trees that stand on it; `TREE.hill(ctx,h)` in phase B before those trees are built.

Marks (round 6, D-13 / B5 tell 4): DRAFT ridge pieces 40–80 px alpha 55–85, shifted ±1 px, a fifth missing; the ridge as **one** `bline` at the primary grade. **The body is one slope from the ridge to a foot that lies flat.** `spec.hk.footY(x)` is the lower edge: a near-horizontal ground level under the whole hill — the ridge's own landing height where the ridge comes down to the ground (`max ridge y + 2`), otherwise `depth` under the crest, capped at 1.6 depth — joined to the ridge by a soft max (softness 0.14 depth) so the lower edge is a curve that becomes horizontal, with ±0.06 depth of slow noise; never a diagonal copy of the ridge. The footprint polygon is ridge ∪ that foot curve. Five folds leave the crest region (spread over 50–80 % of the ridge) and run **down to the foot**, steep near the ridge and lying down within 0.6 depth of the foot (their lateral direction turns toward horizontal by 55–85 %); the 皴 are short hatches (round 9: 4.5–11 px, bowed up to ±1.6 px, no head or tail) that follow the fold's tangent turned 0.4–0.9 rad, rows 2.5–4.5 px apart up to 34 px onto the shaded (left) side of the fold, odds exp(−off/14) falling with the fold's progress (×(1 − 0.65 fade)), each stopped 4 px above the foot — so they lie down toward the foot and thin out before it, and the foot has no line. Fold lines are broken texture marks (pieces 9–22 px, a fifth omitted); all hill 皴, fold, valley and toe marks are ×0.8 alpha (round 9, `HA`), the ridge alone primary; the lit side of each fold gets a few short hatches near the crest only. 点苔 1–1.4 px alpha 180–220 just under the ridge every 10–30 px, 30 % skipped, sometimes doubled; the wash in INDIGO (ink 0.11–0.15 淡墨 or 0.10–0.14 花青) bounded by the ridge polygon, heavier on the shaded side of each fold, dissolving toward the foot through fine noise holes over the body's real depth (`footY − ridge`).

| `weave` | (round 8, D-15) default off. `true` knits the crown: after the sheaves are grown, `weaveTwigs` re-bends every twig whose origin lies inside 0.66 of the crown's radius (centre = mean of twig origins) into an arc turning toward the centre (its direction blended 10–30 % toward the centre at the start and 75–100 % at the end, capped at 1.9 rad, with a small counter-wobble) and 1.25–1.7× as long, so each crosses one or two neighbours; the rim (origins beyond 0.66) keeps one twig in three, unchanged, ending free. Segment–segment crossings of the inner twigs are collected, a sparse pick of them (one in three, none within 4.5 px of another) becomes `sk.crossings`, and that pick is where the dots go: 槐/杂 get 淡墨 leaf dots (0.9–1.3 px, alpha 45–75, INDIGO pass) at 80 % of them, 梧桐 balls come from them instead of every twig end (35 %), 樱/桃 blossom clusters likewise (**round 11**: the crossings are still collected, but the dots, balls and blossom go only where a crossing or twig lies inside one of the crown's leaf masses — see `leafMasses`). Nothing else in the growth changes. **Round 9**: `feature` and `weave` work together on one tree — the knot's twigs are kept as grown (they still count for crossings, so leaf dots land in the knot) and a woven twig whose middle or end would reach into the through-gap (`sk.inGap`) is left unwoven. **main.js**: round 9 — set both `feature` and `weave` on one 晴川阁 tree; `weave` alone on the tree next to the featured 東湖 tree as before. |

Kinds all share one skeleton (**round 6, D-13** — v4's sweep growth with one change): (1) sweep 1 (`sk.sweeps[0]`, arc 0.98–1.08 h, lean with `dir`, S or C bow) is the trunk AND its continuation: the trunk polygon (`sk.S/L/Rt/trunkPoly`, base 0.04–0.062 h wide, root flare, 2–3 节疤, `sk.inner` the shaded left 30 %) runs to the fork at 0.24–0.62 h by kind, and `sk.chains[0]` (role `dom`, geometric width 0.85 wt thinning to nothing) **carries the trunk on up into the crown to 0.85–0.9 h**; (2) the big limbs (`sk.chains[1..]`, `limb:true`) each leave sweep 1 at their **own height** — about 0.3, 0.5, 0.66 h (trees ≥ 115 px: 0.3, 0.48, 0.63, 0.78; trees < 100 px: 0.32, 0.52, 0.68, the top one dropped in four of ten; 松 two or three), jittered ±0.04 h — on **alternating sides**, from the trunk edge below the fork or the chain's half-width above it, at 35–60° from the trunk's tangent there (松 55–75°), each a C-sweep bowing back toward vertical, 0.34–0.46 h long for the lowest and shorter by 55 % of the height fraction above 0.3 h; the lower two are `dom`, the rest `near`, widths 0.6–0.78 wt falling with height. Never two at one node, never a matched pair. (3) Secondary limbs leave every chain at 25–45° (松 nearer horizontal) and bend back; on a big limb the full side is its **upper** side (78 %), on the trunk's continuation the outer side of the bow (70 %); the longer near ones carry a level-3 child. (4) Twig sheaves (5–9 twigs from one origin, 3–5 sparse; small trees −2) sit at every secondary end and child end, straight off the chains (from 12 % along a limb, 25 % along the trunk continuation, 80 % on the full side, 30 % on the other), and at every chain end; 2–3 holes on any chain leave the silk showing. So the crown is the union of the limbs' sheaves and closes as a mass with the trunk visible inside it. `sk.tips` are chain and secondary ends (松 tufts, 柳 sheaves), `sk.twigEnds` the twig ends (梧桐 balls, 樱/桃 blooms). The old `low` trunk limbs are gone (the 0.3-h limb is that limb now).

Leaf methods: 柳 — 垂条 sheaves every 12–20 px along each `dom` chain (55 %, outside holes, bowing the way the chain bows) and from 40 % of the non-sparse tips, so the strands hang from the trunk's continuation and the two lower limbs across the breadth; strands stop 4–12 px above the ground; 汁绿 dots on the hanging ends. 梧桐 — seed balls on 70 % of the twig ends **inside a leaf mass** (round 11; was 45 % of all ends), 淡墨 bark patches. 樱/桃 — `sk.blooms` on 60 % of the mass's twig ends plus a 30 % pick of its inner anchors (≥ 2; round 11 — was 22 % of all ends), up to 8 clusters 淡朱砂, the rest 淡墨. 松 — no twig sheaves; needle tufts along the upper side of each big limb's outer third every 4.5–7 px (60 % doubled above), two fans at the limb tip, one or two at every secondary end and one mid-way on the longer secondaries; tufts alternately dark and pale. 槐/杂 — bare wood; the leaves are the round-11 masses (below).

## Footprint shape

- Bare trees (`huai za wutong ying tao`): the trunk polygon only — the two edge polylines joined (knot bulges and root flare included). Lines behind break at the trunk and nowhere else, as the contract asks.
- `liu`: trunk polygon ∪ one ellipse inscribed in the bounding box of all strands.
- `song`: trunk polygon ∪ one small ellipse per needle cluster (rx 0.95 r, ry 0.85 r, centred a third of a radius above the cluster base) — the needle masses hide what is behind them, the gaps between them do not.
- `rock`: its 8-vertex polygon.
- `luwei`: a 2×1 px box whose `inside` is always false — reeds hide nothing.
- hill (`hillFootprint`): the ridge-to-foot polygon at `z` = ridge top − 1 by default, stamped before the things that stand on it.

## Round 11 (D-21, B10 tell ⑤) — what changed and what the render showed

**Leaves organised by limb: 2–4 masses per crown, closed inside, open between.** `leafMasses(ctx,sk)` runs in the growth (after the crossings, before the balls and blooms) for every kind but 柳 and 松: the candidates are the big limbs (`limb:true`) and the trunk's continuation, the main tree's gathered limbs first, then the longest; trees ≥ 115 px take 3–4 masses, smaller ones 2–3 (a crown with fewer limbs than masses puts two on its longest limb, at 0.5–0.65 and 0.8–0.95). Each mass is an ellipse lying along its limb at 0.55–0.82 of the limb's length, lifted 0.03–0.06 h to the limb's upper side (the trunk continuation: no lift, rounder), rx 0.10–0.145 h (gathered limbs ×0.85, 樱/桃 ×1.2), ry 0.65–0.85 rx. The anchors are every twig crossing (`sk.crossAll`) and, along every twig that reaches a mass, a point every 1.6 px — with one or two more points 1.1–1.7 px beside the twig where it runs inside 0.75 of a mass, so the dots close over the twig network in the heart and string along it at the rim. An anchor is kept with certainty inside 0.6 of the mass radius, with probability falling to zero at the rim (the soft edge), never outside; kept anchors closer than 1.3 px to one already kept are dropped (the dots touch, they do not pile). Results: `sk.masses`, `sk.massDots` [x,y,d], `sk.massEnds` (twig ends inside a mass, for balls and blossom) and `sk.massD(p)` (distance to the nearest mass, in radii; no dice in it — the 汁绿 and 石绿 preds use it at draw time). The dot pass in `drawTree` draws one dot per kept anchor at the grades in the stage table; the old crossing clusters and the "dot or two on a third of the inner twig ends" are gone. 梧桐 balls come from `massEnds` at 70 %; 樱/桃 blossom clusters from `massEnds` plus a 30 % pick of the inner anchors at 60 % (the 朱砂 cap of 8 clusters unchanged, so the red count on the scroll does not move). The 汁绿 for 槐/梧桐 is one wash per mass instead of the inner-half ellipse; the ochre base on the inner half stays; the willow's canopy wash, strands and strand dots are untouched; the hills untouched.

**Counts** (node, the harness scene averaged over eight dice offsets, leaf dots + balls + blossom dots, 柳/松 excluded): v10 3 376 → 2 615, −23 % (槐 761 → 701, 杂 1 525 → 970, 梧桐 578 → 397, 樱 268 → 231, 桃 245 → 193); the first cut (masses 2–4 for every size, blossom at 40 % of mass ends) measured −35 %, and 樱 at −50 %, so the mass count was tied to height and the blossom rate raised — the dots lost are the ones that were between the limbs.

## Round 10 (D-19 (1) (3), B9 tells 1 and 5) — what changed and what the render showed

**Ink into the wood; the crown a mass with holes; colour thin over ochre; one tree biased from the root.** Trunk: the edges run PR ×1.3 wide at the root, alpha PR+25 (was +12), thinning to the fork; every 节疤 ring is structural ×1.3 and its eye is filled with two or three heavy curling strokes (kn.r 0.55–0.95, alpha PR+10..30) — the darkest marks on the trunk; the shaded side is a 淡墨 band 0.42 of the half-width at ink 0.34 (lower third again at 0.22) **and** one 淡墨 woody stroke up that band; the 皴 on the shaded side are 7–12 at structural −25..+10 (were 5–9 at texture).
