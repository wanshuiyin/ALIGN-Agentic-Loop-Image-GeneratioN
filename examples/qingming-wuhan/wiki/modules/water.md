# mod-water.js · 水纹

Files: `src/mod-water.js` (module), `src/scene-water.js` (self-check scene), `src/harness-water.html` (harness.html with the two `<script src>` lines pointed at them), `review/module-water.png` (the render). Contract: `wiki/interfaces.md` §2(e), §3; rules: `wiki/plan.md` §Rules 1–2, 3 (water bands), 8.

## Public function

`WATER.build(ctx, spec) → {fp, slots:{}}` — the only export (no `footprint()`, water never occludes). Every ink mark goes to `ST.WATER`, every colour to `ST.INDIGO`. The returned record is also pushed onto **`ctx.reg.water`** (a list the module creates on first use; `reg` had no slot for water).

### spec

| field | meaning |
|---|---|
| `x0, x1` | horizontal extent of the band, `x0 < x1` |
| `yTop(x), yBot(x)` | far and near bank as functions of x. Off-canvas values are fine (`yTop` returning < 0 for a river lost in the upper mist, `yBot` ≥ `SH` where the near bank is off the bottom) — a bank that is off-canvas simply attracts no clusters |
| `flow` | `[dx,dy]` base current direction (any magnitude; default `[-1,0]`, i.e. toward −x). The per-band field is this vector rotated by a slow noise (±0.4 rad max), bent along a bank's tangent within 50 px of it, along a hull's waterline within 60 px, split around a pier's upstream face (40 px) and reversed in its lee |
| `density` | 0–1, default 1. Scales bank acceptance `(0.7+0.3·density)` only; open water gets at most one current per 400 px at every density (round 4). 1 = banks nearly lined; 0.3 = the S3 open river (banks sparsely lined, hulls wrapped, one current per 400 px); 0 = wakes, hull and pier wraps, the currents |
| `kind` | `river` (everything below), `confluence` (river + `flow2/seam` behaviour + 清浊线), `wake` (no field, no bank strips: only wakes, bow crests, hull-hugging streaks and pier marks — for the S9 wharf and any stretch the plan wants almost bare), `lake` (round 10: only the calm skin — alpha ×0.7, wavelength 44, amplitude 0.35 — no lines, no colour; 东湖. main.js does not yet call it: the lake polygon is stamped but no band is built over it) |
| `piers` | `[{x, y, w}]` — waterline centre and width of each pier (bridge piers, 趸船 posts). Crests pile on the upstream face, 2–3 eddies sit 6–34 px into the lee, the field's flow splits around the pier and runs backwards in its lee so the clusters there oppose the current |
| `flow2` | `confluence` only: the 汉江 current `[dx,dy]` |
| `seam` | `confluence` only: `[x,y]` where the two waters first meet; the seam runs from there along `flow` |
| `seamLen` | length of the seam, default 250 |

### What it reads from `ctx.reg.boats`

Each entry must be `{fp, slots, seats, spec}` with `spec = {x,y,type,len,dir,z,moving}` (the boat's own build spec). The module uses `fp` (x0,x1,y0,y1,z,inside) and `spec.dir / spec.moving / spec.len`. From `fp.inside` it scans the lowest inside pixel in every column — the waterline profile (水线以下不画, so the footprint's bottom edge *is* the waterline) — and from that gets bow, stern, distance-to-hull and hull tangent. Boats whose waterline lies outside the band (±60 px in x, ±10 px in y) are ignored, so a boat is handled by the band it floats in.

### Footprint shape

`fp = {x0, x1, y0: min yTop, y1: max yBot, z: 0, inside(x,y) = x∈[x0,x1] ∧ yTop(x) ≤ y ≤ yBot(x)}` — the band itself. `z` is 0 on purpose: it must **not** be stamped (`stampZ` would clamp it to 1 and hide nothing useful; water lies under everything).

## The marks (all in `ST.WATER`, colour `C.ink`; D-19: a skin under everything, the visible lines rising by contact)

- **skin** (`skin`, round 11, D-21(1); round 10's dashes read as 「木纹状底纹」) — the whole band, every kind (the lake only this, calmer), carries the fine 水纹 the original's river shows edge to edge, as *long lines that follow the flow*. Rows along the band's `flow` (v = across, u = along); each row is a chain of 40–140 px pieces with 3–8 px gaps (×0.7 near a bank or hull), every piece a gentle wave (wavelength 34 px ×0.85–1.15 per row, drifting 0.8–1.2 along the row; amplitude 0.7 px ×0.7–1.3 per row × 0.6–1.3 per piece) on a sheet warped ±3 px by `noise(x·0.004, y·0.015)` plus ±1 px at `(0.012, 0.03)`, so the rows undulate together. 错开接续: every start and end of a row is kept ≥ 10 px from every start and end of the row above (`prevB`; a transition that would land within 10 px is postponed), so ends never stack into a column. Width 0.36–0.44 px, alpha 40–52 (v10: 0.55–0.65 / 60–80 — per-line contrast under half). Density follows the current: row pitch 1.8–2.4 px within 20 px of a bank opening to 2.6–3.4 by 80 px (`smoothstep(20,80,dB)` at the row's mid-point), alpha ×(1+0.4·nearness) within 80 px of a bank and within the 60/40 px hull/pier zones (nearness reaches 0 exactly at the zone edge — an earlier 80-px divisor left a boxed patch round the sampan), and an interleaved half-pitch row that exists only where hull/pier nearness > 0.35+0.4·noise (a ragged, edgeless thickening under hulls and piers); the upper 30 % of the band fades ×0.4 → 1 (`smoothstep(0,0.3,t)`), width ×0.85 → 1. No gap-lengthening noise, so no cluster has an edge. Points are tested like every other water point (band, `masks.hidden`, `clear` 1.6 px from a hull, y ≥ −1). Drawn flat in ~120 px columns, one stroke per column keyed at its rightmost point; the visible lines below draw on top. All dice from `ctx.noise` (z-slots 61–79), none from `ctx.R`. Measured on the harness (silk 124.0): open water 121.9 (−1.7 %), sd 2.8, p10/p90 117/124; bank foot 118.7 (−4.3 %); far zone −1.5 %. v10 measured −2.9 % mid-stream with the dashes; the missing 1 % would need either the v10 alpha or the v10 pitch back, both of which are what B10 objected to — the tone is left at −1.7 %.
- **line profile** — every visible water line takes its width from `ctx.INK.texture` × 1.1–1.45 (round 10, see the contact grades below); no head, no hook, no bead. The end is a gentle two-step thinning (×0.8 then ×0.58 width, alpha ×0.8 then ×0.5) over the last part of the line; where the thinning starts (`tf`) is 0.62 for clusters, 0.6 for hull/pier contours, 0.45 for wake arms. Body width and alpha are otherwise flat.
- **cluster** — one wave family: n = 2–6 lines, each 0.8–1.12 × L (clamped 8–64 px), spacing 1.9–3.4 px with ±12 % per-gap jitter. All lines share one phase (per-line drift ≤ 0.08 rad), one wavelength (±5 %) and one amplitude (±15 %); starts sit on a slanted front (echelon, ±0.22 L per line, not a lens) and the lines diverge slowly toward the downstream end (offsets ×1.04–1.22 by the end). 8 % of inner lines are left out when n > 3. Never a chevron: no per-line curvature sign changes, no alternating wavelengths.
- **current** (round 4, D-10 tell 6) — the only mark open water carries and the construction bank families now use. n lines (4–6 mid-river, 3–5 at a bank), each 25–48 px, the outer ones a fifth shorter than the middle; their starts are staggered *along* the flow (by up to half a line length per line, one sign per current; per-line length clamped 25–64 px) so the group is a long slanting band, not a packet with a front across the current; each line is walked point by point through the field, blending its heading toward the local flow at every step, so all of them bend together (one water moving); one shared slow wave, small amplitude, growing a little downstream; one inner line breaks once for 5–8 px when n > 4; every line thins to nothing downstream (`tail`, tf 0.55–0.6). Three anchors are `put` (centre and both ends) so nothing else starts on it. `current(c, d, {n?, L?, sp?, al, tf?})`; internal only — the field calls it.
- **streak** — 1–2 clusters chained end-to-end along the local flow, the family (phase, echelon, divergence) shared, the line count wandering by one; only the last cluster thins.
- **eddy** — unchanged: inward spiral, 1.1–1.7 turns, flattened ×0.62, 60 % with a companion arc; texture width ×1.1–1.25, alpha 85–115.
- **crest (尖弧)** — 2–3 pointed arcs, 10–14 px across at a bow (8–12 at a pier), stacked 3.2 px apart, alpha 105–130, width ×1.15–1.3.
- **hull contours** (`hullContours`, replaces `hullWrap`) — 3–5 long contours per hull, 24–60 px each (≤ 0.7 × hull length), following the waterline profile as offset curves. Offsets start 2.4–3.4 px under the hull and open by 3–4.2 px, growing ×1.35 per row; the stack is compressed when a moored hull has little water under it before the bank. Contours alternate their anchor: one closes against the stern, the next against the bow (a moored hull's end is rounded as a quarter-circle; a moving hull's contour starts on the raked bow 2.5 px in, or on the stern where the wake takes over), run inward along the hull, and over their last 45 % release — the offset opens by 3–9 px (quadratic), a slow noise bends the line, and it thins to nothing. A third of contours carry one 4–8 px break. Alpha 135–170 fading a fifth by the outermost row, width ×1.25–1.45 (round 10: the darkest water is against the hull, as in the reference). Blank silk between contours; `h.wrapR` = 60 so the field never enters the hull zone. Points closer than 0.6 o + 0.6 to the hull are dropped, and **every water point is dropped within 1.6 px of any hull's waterline** (`clear`).
- **wake** (moving boats) — two arms leaving the stern at ±0.15–0.24 rad, direction −motion blended with 0.5 × the lightly noised current, length 0.7–1.05 × boat length (≤ 210). Each arm is 2–3 long lines 2.2–3.2 px apart sharing one gentle wave (amplitude 0.5–0.9, wavelength 30–60), opening apart ×2.6 by the end; the first line runs 80–100 % of the arm, the others 40–75 %, half of them broken once for 6–12 px; alpha 110–140, width ×1.1–1.3, all thinning to nothing from 45 % on. No churn arcs, no stern eddy. A crest ahead of the bow. The field leaves the wake wedge and the bow wedge blank.
- **pier** (`pierMarks`) — a crest on the upstream face; 3–4 contours round the foot at offsets 2.4–3.4 → +2.6–3.6 per row (×1.3), each running from the upstream corner over the foot and 10–22 px past the downstream corner, releasing along the current and thinning, alpha 130–160, width ×1.2–1.4; one eddy 16–36 px into the lee.
- **field** (round 4) — candidates on a 7 px jittered grid, random order. Only bank nearness counts: candidates within 0.9 × 60 px of a hull or 0.9 × 40 px of a pier are skipped (those zones carry their contours and nothing else). A bank family is accepted only where w > 0.3 — within 35 px of the near bank, 25 px of the far bank (weighted 0.6) — never adrift between; it is one `current` of 3–5 lines, L 36–52 px (× depth 0.8–1; round 11 — was 26–40), spacing 2.6–3.4 px pressing to 1.9–2.4 against the bank, alpha 56–82 (round 11 — was 80–116: on the calmer skin the old families stood as short dark clumps, the very 短线簇 B10 named; width ×1.1–1.3), with probability `(0.42+0.16w)·(0.7+0.3·density)·(0.5+0.5·bankQuiet)`, spacing `54−12w` from anything placed. Open water carries nothing but **at most one current per 400 px** (`round((x1−x0)/400)` of them, so a band under 200 px gets none): 4–6 lines, L 40–56 px (× depth; round 11 — was 30–40), alpha 52–72 (was 72–100), at 45–85 % of the way to the near bank, ≥ 90 px from anything placed, ≥ 60 px from any hull, only where no bank is felt (w ≤ 0.02). `current`'s per-line clamp is 25–64 px. The 2–3-line drifts and the streaks of short clusters are no longer placed anywhere (`cluster`/`streak` remain in the file, unused by the field).
- **clipping** — every stroke point is tested against the band and `masks.hidden(x,y,y)` (its own depth); hidden points are dropped and the visible runs (≥ 3 points) drawn. Hulls, piers, decks, bank objects and figures stamped into Z therefore cut water without any fill; the 1-px waterline row of a hull polygon is not hidden (Z = z, not > z+1) and is covered by the boat's own bottom line.

## Colour (`ST.INDIGO`)

- **Bank foot rows** (`river`, `confluence`; D-15 b): stretches of 140–300 px every 160–420 px along the near bank, two rows each, offset 2.5–5 and 5.5–10 px into the water (always within 12 px of the bank foot), widths 2.6–4 / 1.8–2.8 px, alpha 26–36 / 22–30 (ink ≈ 0.10–0.14), 淡花青. Each row is a chain of 40–90 px pieces with 25–70 px gaps, the second row starting 10–40 px later than the first — never a continuous band; a hull over a row breaks it too. No INDIGO anywhere else on the water.
- **清浊线** (`confluence`; D-15 b): two or three broken lines along the seam, 3–5 px apart, 淡赭 and 淡花青 alternating, texture width (×1.05–1.25), alpha 88–112 (the outer lines ×0.88), each a chain of 60–120 px pieces with 20–55 px gaps, wavering with the seam's ±6 px noise, alpha halving between 0.6 and 1.1 × seamLen. At the head a feather 10–14 px across — a few 淡赭 dabs on the 长江 side and 淡花青 on the 汉江 side at ink 0.09, each crossing the seam by ~3 px — is the only place the two colours mingle. The dab bands (26 px wide, ink 0.12–0.16) are gone. The field's current on the 汉江 side is still `flow2` at the mouth turning into `flow` over 1.3 × seamLen.

## Deviations from interfaces §2(e)

- `kind`, `piers`, `flow2`, `seam`, `seamLen` are new spec fields (the brief for this module asked for `kind`; piers and the seam have no other source — the bridge's piers are not in the arch module's slot list, so the integrator passes their waterline centres).
- `ctx.reg.water` is new; `reg.zones` is left to the crowd module.
- The 清浊线 is lines, not a tinted face (B7 tell 5 overrode B1's ink 0.12–0.16 band); the bank rows are ink 0.10–0.14 within 12 px of the foot.
- Nearness zones are 60 / 50 / 40 px (hull / bank / pier), not plan §Rules' 40: B1 found the hulls unanchored, and the wrap needs the room to open from 1.2 px to 2.8 px spacing.
- Streaks: the contract's "cluster gap 10–40 px" is kept between streaks; within a streak clusters abut, because the original's groups are 30–80 px long.

## Integration notes

- Bands must not overlap: each `build` has its own spacing grid, so overlapping bands double the density. Give the 汉江 its own band (kind `river`, its own `flow`) up to the mouth and let the 长江 band at S7 be the `confluence` with `flow2` = the 汉江 direction and `seam` = the mouth's downstream corner (plan: `(1920,140)`, `seamLen` 250).
- `yTop/yBot` are called with any x in `[x0−4, x1+4]` (tangent probes), so make them total functions.
- Order in `build()`: after all footprints are stamped and after `BOAT.build` has pushed every boat (the wake needs `spec.moving`).
- Stroke-object budget: the 1200 × 250 harness band produced 374 objects at density 1 and 346 at 0.3 (banks and hulls dominate; each hull wrap and each pier wrap is one object); a 3000 × 200 river with 20 boats lands around 1000–1200.
