# mod-arch.js · 界画 architecture

Files: `src/mod-arch.js` (module, one ES5 IIFE `ARCH`), `src/scene-arch.js` (self-check scene), `src/harness-arch.html` (harness pointed at both), `review/module-arch.png` (the render). Contract: `wiki/interfaces.md` §2(a), §3; rules: `wiki/plan.md` §Rules. Deviations from interfaces.md are marked **≠**.

## Public functions

```js
ARCH.footprint(ctx, spec)  → {x0,x1,y0,y1,z,inside(x,y),polys}     // pure: no ctx.add, no ctx.R
ARCH.build(ctx, spec)      → {fp, slots, kind}                        // draws; pushes the same record into ctx.reg.buildings
```
`spec.kind` selects the builder; **≠** the Song building is `kind:'hall'` (the default when `kind` is absent). Geometry is computed by the same function in both calls and never consumes `ctx.R`, so the stamped footprint and the drawn building are the same shape; all randomness is in the drawing and is captured in the stroke closures. Text is never rasterised here — sign kinds return a `signRect`/`huangzi` slot carrying `spec.sign` for `TEXT.sign`.

### Common spec fields
| field | meaning |
|---|---|
| `x,y` | ground contact of the front-right corner (bridge: right end of the truss at deck level; steps: right end of the first tread) |
| `w` | frontage, extends to the left of `x` |
| `d` | depth along `ctx.OBL` (default `0.6·w`) |
| `h` | ground-to-eave height (towers: total height) |
| `dir` | `1` (default): depth vector = `OBL`, the **left** flank shows. **≠** `-1` mirrors the x component of `OBL` so the right flank shows (a street receding the other way) |
| `z` | defaults to `y` |
| `pale` | **D-08** multiplier on every ink alpha and wash of the building (default 1) — main.js's roof stacks give each successive roof behind `pale: 1 − 0.1·k` |

Doors: the upper 45 % 0.34 + 0.12 over the leaf; counters: interior band 0.34, lower 0.14. ### `bridge` (长江大桥, plan S5)
`{x,y, spans 9, span 80, rise, trussH0 28 → trussH1 22, dir, z, water (y at the piers, default y+70 — a number, or a function of x so the water climbs with a climbing deck; main.js passes `yD(x)+66`), first/total, fadeFrom 5, approach {right,left}, pavilion {right,left}, railZ (D-08, default z+80)}`.

**D-08 deck and rail.** The deck's top edge is the receiving plane: one primary line along `yD(x)`, a 淡赭 over the road surface between it and the far kerb, a 淡墨 shadow line under it; the truss (chords, verticals, diagonals) hangs entirely below that line. The far side is only the far kerb and the far rail top, both structural and paler — nothing of the far truss shows above the deck. The near 栏杆 (top rail primary, posts every ~8 px down to the deck edge) is drawn at `railZ`, not at the bridge's z, and has its own footprint: `ARCH.footprint(ctx, bridgeSpec)` returns the usual fp **plus `fp.rail`** (a thin strip from the rail top to just above the deck edge, plus the approach rails, at `railZ`) — main.js stamps both, `stampZ(fp); stampZ(fp.rail)`. Deck figures then take a z strictly between the bridge's z and `railZ`, feet on `slots.deckZone.yAt(x)+0.5` (= `seats[].y`): the rail strip hides their legs, they hide the far rail, and nothing of them reaches the truss. `slots.railLine = [{x,y}…]` (sorted by x, one per post, the rail top) for hands and elbows; `slots.railFp` is the same fp as `fp.rail`; `slots.railZ`.
The truss face is drawn span by span: top and bottom chords 0.9 px / alpha 195, verticals every `span/round(span/13)` ≈ 13 px at 0.7 / 165, Warren diagonals zig-zagging between them at 0.5 / 135; 18 % of verticals and diagonals are drawn with a 2.5–4 px gap (the brush lifted). The far truss shows as its top chord and railing 14 px of depth up-left, with the tops of its verticals. Road deck: rail 4 px above the top chord with posts every 7.5–8.5 px; rail deck: **round 10** — a continuous primary line at `yB − 5.5`, parapet at `yB − 9.5` with posts, a shadow under the slab (was two pale lines). Spans with index `first+i ≥ fadeFrom` fade linearly to alpha 40 % at the last span of `total`; `first/total` let a piece of the bridge be built (the harness scene is spans 1–3 of 9) with the right fade and pier sizes. Piers at every span joint: front face 14 → 10 px wide with flank, broken course pieces with staggered joints, 分水尖 on the upstream (left) side, 淡赭 and a wet 淡墨 foot. 引桥 at either end where the piece is the end of the whole bridge (or `approach.right/left` arches): deck and railing continue, spandrel wall 34 px with stilted arches 22 px wide, keystone tick, 淡墨 in the openings. 桥头堡 at those ends: **round 10 (D-18)** — twin 4-tier square towers ≈ 122 px + cap, see the Round 10 section (the 7-tier thin pagoda is gone). Slots: `deckZone {x0,x1,yAt(x),railY(x),z,dir}`, `railDeck {yAt(x)}`, `seats` every 9–14 px along the near rail `{x,y,z,dir,rail:true}` for figures leaning over, `piers [{x,top,bot,w}]`, `pavilions [{x,y}]`.

### `streetRow` — 連續街屋 (D-10, round 4)
```js
ARCH.streetRowFootprint(ctx, spec) → fp            // phase A: ctx.masks.stampZ(fp)
ARCH.streetRow(ctx, spec)          → {fp, slots, kind:'streetRow:<street>'}   // phase B
// or through the generic pair: ARCH.footprint / ARCH.build with {kind:'streetRow', row:'hubuxiang'}
spec = {x,y,w, z, dir, kind:'hubuxiang'|'hanzhengjie'|'lilong'|'jianghanlu'|'hzrear', h, depth, rise, signs:[...], pale}   // hzrear: round 9, the rear rank as one mass
slots = {signRects:[…], eaveLines:[{y,x0,x1,unit}], counters:[…], doors:[…], huangzi:[…], gates:[{x,y,w,h}], units:[{kind,x0,x1,y,setback,z}], ground}
```
One row is one structure (`rowPlan`, noise only, so footprint and build agree). `x,y` = ground contact of the right end, `w` the whole frontage, `h` the two-storey eave height (defaults per street), `depth` the second rank's distance, `rise` the total climb of the street edge from right to left (0). 5–9 units in a sequence: rightmost a full shop; two-storey shops (悬山/硬山, counters, a sign from `signs` in order, 幌子 on every other), single-storey shops (lean-to or 悬山, 凉棚), one gate/passage in the middle third (a tiled wall flush with the front line, a dark opening with the 里弄's rear wall courses and one small window seen through it), and at the left end a corner unit turned along the side street (narrow front, deep flank, gable to the viewer, red-edged 幌子). `jianghanlu` mixes 租界 colonnade units in; `lilong` is 石库门 units (each the existing `lilong` wall + pair + 天井) with a gate between pairs and no corner unit. The front line steps in and out along OBL by 4–14 px (at least two steps back and two forward); units on the same line attach (no 翘角 lift between them, party wall); a unit whose left neighbour is set back shows its flank as a 淡墨 wall face (`flankShade`) with the eave's return over it; at every step a structural return joins the two ground lines and the recess floor carries a 淡墨 strip. Under the eave: a 2–3 px 淡墨 band right under the eave line, and (`street:true` hall units only) a darker interior band over the upper part of every counter bay and doorway. Second rank: roof-only halls (grey/花青, `pale ×0.9`) behind the gate, the low units and a few others, set back by `depth` + a little, each with the whole front row as local occluders, so they show only above the lower units and in the gaps. Ranks: smaller setback = nearer; ties: the left unit hides the right one's flank; each unit's local occluders are the nearer units within two places. The footprint is the union of everything (fronts with eaves and 翘角, flanks, gate wall, second-rank roofs).
New hall spec fields (used by the row, harmless elsewhere): `street:true` (interior bands, eave band), `flankShade` (淡墨 ink on the flank). `lilongDraw` and `colonnadeDraw` now honour `G.occ` like `drawHall`.
Render (`review/module-arch.png`, crops `_arch_rows25.png`, `_arch_lilong25.png`, `_arch_jhl25.png`): the 戶部巷 row at 1× reads as a stepped street of unequal heights with a gate and rear roofs peeking, not a scaled repeat; at 2.5× the set-back two-storey shop shows a shaded flank under its eave return, the gate is a dark slot with a roof behind, the counters are dark under the 额枋. The 漢正街 row behind at 40 % overlap is cut by the front row's silhouette. Still true: upper-storey lattice windows repeat within a row; the recess floors are bare silk apart from the strip.

## Round 11 (D-21 · B10 ③④⑤) — the 桥头堡 is a block on its abutment, the train rides inside the truss, the 黄鹤楼's main storey has depth

**桥头堡 (`BH`, `bhGeom`, `towerAt`).** One geometry helper feeds both the footprint and the drawing. Tiers 40/26/22/16 px on half-widths 35/32/29.5/27 (eave overhang 2.2, the pavilion 3), tile bands 3.6, a 攒尖 cap of 10 px + 宝顶 — 128 px above the deck, 70 px wide at the base (0.55). The ground tier does not stop at the deck: the block continues 32 px (`ab`) below it as the abutment, one wall of rusticated courses (texture grade, staggered joints) from the tier's eave to the ground, the deck level only a 0.7-px string course across the face; two arched openings (8 × 18, 淡墨 0.34 inside) stand on the deck; the corner edges primary with an inner pilaster line; the left flank 11 px deep at 0.36 over the whole height; 淡赭 0.2 over tier and abutment alike. Tiers 2–3 carry three windows each, the fourth is the open pavilion (six columns, 栏杆, 0.38 inside). On the near block's **outer** side (away from the truss: `+dir` at the bridge's start, `−dir` at its end) a flight of 石阶 in profile climbs the abutment: 12 treads as a sawtooth edge (structural, nosings heavier as they descend), the wall under it with two or three courses, 淡赭, 淡墨 at its foot, the ground line primary; the stair is a polygon of its own (`pp.stair`, in `polys`, so main.js's stamp hides what is behind it) and occludes the flank where the flight stands in front of it (the end where the flank side is the outer side). The far twin (14 px of depth behind, pale 0.62) is cut by the near block and its stair; it no longer occludes the truss — the truss is in front of it.

**Rail deck and train.** The parapet-and-posts of round 10 are gone. The lower chord `yB` stays primary; the rail surface is a structural line at `yB − 3.5`, the slab between them 淡墨 0.16 — one layer. `spec.train {x, n}` (main.js: `{x:2848, n:7}`, replacing its own `train()` in `extras`) draws the cars in the bridge's stage: body from `yB − 13` (roof, under the road deck by 9–14 px) to `yB − 5.1`, roofline 195/0.8, sill and ends 175–180, window ticks texture, four wheels a car (r 1.3, the lower half-circle and a dot) with their rims ON the rail surface, the body's 淡墨 0.3 gathering toward each car's left end (light from the upper right, as main's `washFace`). Every truss vertical and diagonal whose x-range overlaps the train leaves a 1.6-px strip that the train's rules, ticks and wash are split around — the member in front, the train inside. `slots.railDeck.yAt` is now the rail surface (`yB − 3.5`, was `yB − 5`) and `slots.railDeck.chord = yB`.

`tools(ctx,z,spec)` reads `ctx.INK` (core exports `{primary:[w,al],structural:[w,al],texture:[w,al]}`; falls back to D-08's 0.65/205, 0.5/150, 0.35/90) and offers `T.pri`, `T.str` (ruled, ± a few % alpha per line, `k` scales both) and `T.txt` (texture; a line over 7 px is drawn as runs of 7–16 px with 2–4 px gaps ≈ 80 % of its length, `whole:true` keeps it unbroken). Assignment in the hall: ground line, 额枋, corner columns, eave curves, ridge, the far 垂脊 (the roof's silhouette) and the 石库门 jambs are primary; 台基 upper line, interior columns, window and door frames, flank lines, gable/hip 垂脊, 平座 rails, the ridge's second line are structural; 直棂 bars, 斗拱, 柱础 second line, 瓦垄 (a third of them lift off for 2–4 px on the way up), brick courses, stone joints, dentils, 滴水 drops are texture. Flank and rear lines are one step paler than their front counterparts (rear corner post structural, rear base vertical ×0.8); the rear roof slope is never drawn. `courses` and `cornice` use the same grades; the bridge, 江汉关 tower, 电视塔 and 趸船 keep their explicit numbers (already three-tiered).

## Footprint shape
`unionFp` — a list of polygons tested with `ctx.polyInside`, one `inside` that returns true if any polygon contains the point, and the union bbox. Per kind: hall = 台基 front + flank, wall front + flank, roof front slope — **D-08: the full silhouette**: eave overhang and 翘角 (the lifted `eavePts`), the ridge with its second line and the 鸱吻 on top, the eave zone (rafters / 瓦当 / 斗拱) between the eave and the wall top, front and flank —, visible flank slope, gable, awning mat, the 幌子 banner; towers = every tier's polygons; 江汉关 = block + parapet + the three tower tiers with flanks + cupola; 里份 = wall (with cap) + wall flank + house polygons; bridge = the truss band (deck −5 px to bottom chord), the deck between near and far top chords, pier fronts and flanks, approach walls, pavilion trapezoids; 电视塔 = shaft trapezoids and the disc ellipse; wharf = hull, cabin, roof, gangway band (the railing is open and not stamped); wall/embankment = face + flank; steps = the band of treads; 牌坊 = the four column strips, the beam zones and the three small roofs (the bays stay open for people to pass). Balcony rails and bridge railings are not stamped, so a figure at a rail is drawn over it.

## Self-occlusion
The z-mask cannot hide one part of a building behind another part of the same building (same z). `tools(ctx,z)` therefore carries a local occluder list: every ruled line, curve, dot row, wash and 复勾 is split around those polygons before `rline` clips it against the z-mask. Used by: tower tiers (the tier above hides the ridge and 鸱吻 of the one below), 江汉关 (the tower body hides the cornice and parapet behind it), 里份 (the front wall hides the house's ground storey), bridge (the 桥头堡 hides the truss members behind it). `T.clearOcc()` drops the list before the occluding part is drawn itself.
