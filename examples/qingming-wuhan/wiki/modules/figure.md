Folds are texture and there are exactly two (three on a stoop): from the supporting hip toward the opposite shoulder, and a short one from the raised shoulder toward the waist.
- **Head**: sits on a neck 0.028 half-wide that runs from the shoulder bar to the head base and leans with the spine; the loop crosses under the head and that run is hidden by the head disc. Hair/hat/ticks follow the head's turn as before (`head()` unchanged).

The near arm is part of the loop; a frontal figure gets both arms in the loop; a three-quarter/profile far arm is drawn as its own tube, clipped by the torso, legs and head, only when its hand is out in front, above the shoulder, behind the back, or the pose says `showB`. `armTo`/`arm` keep their signatures for the seated and riding bodies (`sitter`, `squat`, `rideEbike`, `sanlun`, `mahjong`) and now emit the same closed sleeve.

A group is **one drawing**: several bodies built from one skeleton system, sharing exact contact points. ### Body construction (round 3, D-08 / B2 tell 2)

Every standing figure is built on a weight-bearing stance, not an upright tube. At build time the figure takes a **supporting leg** (`P.wt`, front or back foot; walkers plant the forward foot in `walk1`, the far foot in `walk2`, `porter` the leg under the sack, `recoil`/`yield` the rear leg, `block`/`biandan`/the exchanging pairs the front leg): that leg runs straight from the hem to its foot, the hips (the hem centre) shift over that foot, the free leg bends at the knee and may lift its heel, and the shoulder line counter-tilts 2–4° (the shoulder above the supporting leg drops). The body axis `ax(v)` runs from the shoulders (on the lean) to the hem (over the foot); the garment, folds, belt, bag and backpack all follow it.

**Three body turns** (`P.turn`: three-quarter on 60 % of figures, frontal 20 %, profile 20 %; a pose may fix its own): frontal has full shoulder width and two eye ticks; three-quarter narrows the shoulders to 0.84, brings the near shoulder forward at the chest, hides the far upper arm behind the torso (only the forearm shows from the elbow), and puts the eye and nose ticks on the near side; profile halves the shoulders, narrows the hem, drops the far arm unless the pose reaches with it, and draws the nose at the face edge with an ear behind. `look:'back'` (used by `yield`) flips the whole head — hair, hat, ticks — to look over the shoulder. Walkers are three-quarter or profile only.

**Feet**: both shoe marks touch the ground line `y`; a lifted heel keeps its toe down; in three-quarter view the far (back) foot sits ~1 px higher than the near one. Robes now end at 0.13 h with bare shins to the shoes, so no feet float under a hem. **Arms**: the reach is capped at 90 % of the two bones, so every elbow bends; angle-driven arms have a minimum forearm bend.
