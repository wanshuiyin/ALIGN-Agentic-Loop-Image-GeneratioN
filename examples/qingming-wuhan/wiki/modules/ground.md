# mod-ground.js · roads, ground, walls, quays, fields, the 凌波門 pier

Files: `src/mod-ground.js` (the module, one ES5 IIFE `GROUND`), `src/scene-ground.js` (self-check scene), `src/harness-ground.html` (harness.html with the two `<script src>` lines pointed at these), `review/module-ground.png` (second render). Contract reference: `wiki/interfaces.md` §2(f), §3.

## Public functions

```
GROUND.footprint(ctx, {zone})  → {x0,x1,y0,y1,z,inside[,slices]}   pure, no add()
GROUND.build(ctx, {zone})      → {fp, slots}                        draws through ctx primitives; pushes {kind,fp,slots} into ctx.reg.zones
GROUND.terrace(ctx, spec)      → {fp:EMPTY, slots:{front,back,poly}}   D-07 (a): one strip of continuous ground, see below
GROUND.steps(ctx, spec)        → {fp:EMPTY, slots:{top,bottom,w}}      D-07 (b): a ruled flight of 3-px treads down to the water
GROUND.bankLine(ctx, spec)     → {fp:EMPTY, slots:{pts}}               D-07 (c): the primary water-edge line with its 淡墨 shadow
GROUND.foot(ctx, {x,y,w,z})    → {fp:EMPTY, slots:{x,y,w}}              D-08: a tree's foot — a mound with 2–3 皴 and a shadow dot (main.js calls it under every tree on a terrace)
```
The three D-07 primitives are also zone kinds (`terrace`, `steps`, `bankline`) so `zone()` in main.js can list them; they draw nothing solid and never stamp.

**Wall footprints carry `slices` (round 2).** A `floodwall`/`wall` of any length or recession is one zone: its base is cut into slices of ≤16 px of y, each with its own z (its near base y), every mark takes the z of the slice it lies in, and `footprint()` returns the whole face plus `slices:[{x0,x1,y0,y1,z,inside}]`. main.js stamps each slice: `var fp=GROUND.footprint(ctx,{zone:z}), sl=fp.slices||[fp]; for(j…)ctx.masks.stampZ(sl[j]);` — stamping only `fp` (one z = the far base y) makes the wall hide its own near part. The five 48-px floodwall pieces of S7 become one `{kind:'floodwall', pts:[[LEx(262)-4,262],[LEx(502)-4,502]], h:36, gates:[…]}`.
`spec.zone` is the zone record; a bare zone record also works (`spec.zone||spec`). `zone.kind` selects the builder. Every polyline that a face hangs from (`quay.front`, `wall.pts`, `floodwall.pts`) must be **x-monotonic** — a front seen from the water or street side; either direction is accepted and normalised to x-ascending.

## Round 10 (D-19 3) · 赭 into the earth

The B9 tone ruling for the ground: 「物象未沉、色层未足」, ground 5–8 % darker and warmer, colour visible but transparent, 「线骨始终可见」. What changed, in craft terms: every terrace strip now carries a full thin 赭石 over its whole depth and a second, darker local 赭 pass at its foot — the foot of a row's wall, a tree's mound, the water's edge — with the reserves kept blank in both; a bank's grass verge takes a thin 汁绿 over the ochre so the green sits on an earth base; a stone quay's face is one thin 赭 with its 淡墨 courses coming back in the lower half, darkest at the water line, and a wet foot. No shape, edge line, 皴, contour or 点苔 changed; the ink skeleton is untouched.

Measured on the harness (canvas at page (51,75); silk after the core's D-19 darkening L 130.9, rgb 150/128/94): street strip L 122.3, bank strip 123.2 (−6 %), quay face 121.4 (−7 %); the bank's foot band rgb 140/110/75 L 115 — the reference bank in `reference/detail_river.png` measures 138/110/77 L 114.6 against its water at 126. Before the round: bank 136.5 against silk 141 (−3 %), the wash an orange fringe at the edge and nothing behind it.

What I looked at (1× beside the reference bank and `detail_city.png`'s ground, 2.5× and 5× crops): the strips read as bands of toned earth with a darker foot and a torn far boundary; the trees stand in darker feet; the verge's grey-green is a veil, not a slope of green; the quay reads wet at the water line with the ink courses above still fading per D-08 (d). First pass used `C.ochre` for the full wash and measured only 1.5 % darker — `C.ochre` has the darkened silk's luminance, so it was swapped for `C.ochre2` and the strips sank to the target. Then the full wash ended on the strip's ruled back line where nothing stood behind it — its back quarter now thins to half with a noise boundary.

`scene-ground.js`: the quay terrace now stops at x 1000 and a `quay` zone (front 1000→1200, wallH 22, one flight of 8 steps, a 14-px platform wash) stands at the right so the 淡墨 courses are exercised; the stand-in water clusters there start below its foot. `main.js` need change nothing: signatures, slots and footprints are unchanged; the `ink` values it passes (0.1–0.28) map to base ×0.75 / foot ×1.25. Core should add `C.zhilv` (a muted 汁绿, e.g. [108,124,70]); the module falls back to that value until it exists.

## D-07 primitives · continuous ground (round 2)

The B1 tell: 「Trees rise from nothing and architecture floats instead of sitting on streets, banks and terraces … stacked rows separated by unarticulated brown fields.」 The answer is one strip per level, chained back to front so the eye steps down street → 防洪墙 → quay → bank → water without a gap, each strip owning its front edge, its own bounded earth wash and the marks that say what it is made of.

`GROUND.terrace(ctx,{pts, depth=24, z=0, kind='street'|'quay'|'bank', ink, tone, plankDir})` — `pts` is the **front edge, given right→left** (normalised x-ascending); the strip lies `depth` px behind it (smaller y); **negative depth** puts the strip in front of the edge (the rows' feet and the river banks in main.js, where the water lies behind the streets) and every mark below takes the sign correctly.
- Front edge (D-08 a): a pale `DRAFT` 起稿 of the given line first, then **one continuous structural-grade line** (`ctx.INK.structural`, `bline`) that dips and rises — 4–8 px by slow noise on a bank, ~1 px on a kerb or 堤石 — unbroken (one break on a bank longer than 300 px); `JIEHUA` for street/quay, `TREES` for bank. The wavy edge is the boundary the wash and the edge marks use; `slots.edge` returns it, `slots.front` stays the given line.
- Contour lines: 2–3 per 160 px on a bank (about one per 300 px on a street or quay), texture grade, 30–60 px long, following the edge at 0.2–0.75 of the depth with a ±1 px wander; 点苔 clusters on the edge every 40–80 px (bank) or 120–200 px (built edges).
- Earth (D-19 3, replacing D-08 b's single graded wash): **two** `OCHRE` dabs calls over the strip with the same 1–2 large irregular reserves (r up to 0.6·depth, noise outline, 3-px soft rim), never on `waterAt` cells, anything stamped nearer cuts them. First a **full thin 赭 over the whole strip** — nominal 0.18 (`spec.ink`×0.75 when given), `C.ochre2`, probability ~0.9 everywhere, thinning to about half over the back quarter so the far boundary is torn, not ruled. Then a **second local pass** — nominal 0.3 (`spec.ink`×1.25), `C.ochre2` — full at the given edge (a row's wall foot for `feet()` strips, the water's edge for banks) and gone at ~45 % of the depth, the boundary wandering ±15 % by noise. Both laid at 2.2× the nominal because dabs' radial fade leaves about half on the silk. `C.ochre2` for both: after D-19's darker silk `C.ochre` (172/120/72) has the silk's own luminance and only warms — it cannot sink the ground.
- Bank only: a 汁绿 verge — `C.zhilv` (fallback [108,124,70] until core defines it) at 0.12 in `INDIGO`, over the ochre base on the far side of the strip from the water's edge (from ~35 % to 100 % of the depth, noise boundary), holes kept. Street and quay strips get no green.
- Street: 沿石 — the kerb face as ruled chords 2 px below the wavy edge in 30–60 px pieces (alpha 95–125), joint ticks 2.4 px tall every 12–18 px (20 % missing).
- Quay: one stone course 6 px tall hung from the edge (chords in pieces, joints every 12–16 px, a quarter missing), 2–3 缆桩 (a 2–2.8 × 4–5.5 px post, line pair + cap, `ochre2`), one 跳板 sloping 12–18 px down to the water (two rails, 3–4 rungs), direction `plankDir` or random.
- Bank (D-08 d): 皴 as **long strokes from the top of the slope down to the water** — groups of 2–4 every 28–44 px (about three per 40 px), each starting 45–70 % of the depth up the slope and ending on the water edge, all leaning one way per strip (slope ×0.8–1.25 per stroke, a whisper of bow), texture grade with the first of a group sometimes a shade heavier — no head, no bead; a few 2.5–4 px stubs on the edge every 15–30 px; 3–5 reed tufts on the edge, 4–6 blades 6–11 px tall fanning ±3 px (alpha 150–200, w 0.48–0.6).
- Returns `slots:{front, back, poly, edge}`; footprint `EMPTY`.

`GROUND.foot(ctx,{x,y,w=5,z=y})` (D-08 c) — `(x,y)` the trunk's ground contact, `w` the trunk width there, `z` the tree's z. A small mound of earth: two structural-grade arcs (`TREES`) from the ground line up into the trunk flare left and right, 2–3 texture-grade 皴 lying on the mound sloping outward to its toe, an `ochre2` wash 0.3 under the mound reaching 35 % past its toe (D-19: the local 赭 pass at a tree's foot), and one heavy shadow dot (`FINISH`, r 0.7–1) beside the trunk on the shaded side. Marks carry the tree's z, so the trunk stamp does not cut them and anything nearer does. Call it after the terrace and before or after the tree — the stages sort by x anyway.

`GROUND.steps(ctx,{x,y,w=30,n=8,z=y+3n,dir=0})` — a flight from the top tread centred at `(x,y)` down `n` treads of 3 px toward the water; treads drift `dir`·0.6 px per step and widen 3 %/step; flank lines structural (alpha 150–172, w 0.62), treads alpha 118–150 w 0.5–0.58 (the top one darker), 15 % of inner treads cut short at one end; `OCHRE` 0.16 over the flight, `INDIGO` 淡墨 0.12 on the lowest two treads (wet). `slots:{top,bottom,w}`.

`GROUND.bankLine(ctx,{pts,z=0})` — the water edge as one primary-grade `bline` (alpha 198–218, w 0.72–0.86, `TREES`) resampled at 2 px with 2–3 breaks, and a 淡墨 shadow line 2 px below it (`C.danmo`, alpha 95–125, w 0.5–0.6, `INDIGO`) with 1–2 breaks and ±0.4 px waver. Pass the joined quay-foot + bank polyline so the line is one stroke across the change of material.

**Railing** (quay `rail`, `pier`, kind `railing`): posts every 7.4–8.6 px (`rline` w 0.7, alpha 140–185, height ×0.96–1.04), a 望柱 every 4–6 posts (w 0.9, 18 % taller, a knob dot), top rail (alpha 190, w 0.8) and a mid rail at 55 % (alpha 130, w 0.55) that is split with a gap in 15 % of runs. Each post carries `z = its foot y + 1`, so figures standing behind it on the platform are cut by it and figures in front cut it.

**Stone courses** (quay face, floodwall): course lines every 5.4–6.6 px measured down from the top line, drawn as 30–60 px pieces with 15 % missing (alpha 0.65–1 × 140, w 0.5–0.6); joints one course tall every 16–30 px with a 7-px stagger on alternate courses, 45 % missing (alpha 0.6–0.85 × 140, w 0.5). **D-08 (d): the courses are drawn only in the front fifth of the face, then fade** — beyond 20 % of the height the kept fraction, the alpha and the joint count all fall to nothing at 80 %, so a 28-px quay face shows one full course, a paler second and traces of a third. Both skip the steps / gate regions. `courses()` takes `z` as a number or as a function of x (the wall's per-slice z).

**Slots returned** (also in `ctx.reg.zones[i].slots`): `quay` → `{front, water (front+wallH), steps:[{x,w,n,yT,yB}], rail:[runs]}`; `floodwall`/`wall` → `{base, top, gate}`; `pier` → `{seats:[{x,y,z,dir}] every 8.5–10.5 px along the near deck edge, deck:[x0,y0,x1,y1], thick}`; `busstop` → `{stop:[x,y]}`; `road` → `{poly, crossings, treepits, busstop}`; `treepit` → `{tree:[x,y]}`; `field`/`ground` → `{poly}`.

## Stage placement

`DRAFT`: road edges, bunds, base lines of walls, water line of the quay, pier axis. `JIEHUA`: everything ruled — 堤石 edges, courses, 台阶, railings, coping, wall cap and 瓦垄/瓦当, 墙墩, gates, tree-pit kerbs, bus-stop pole and board, pier timber. `TREES`: 田垄. `OCHRE`: ground/field/margin washes, faces, steps, deck, posts, tree-pit soil, the crossing band. `INDIGO`: 淡墨 damp/shadow bands, 花青 on the wall cap and the bus-stop header, 淡墨 in gate openings. `FINISH`: 点苔, 复勾 of coping/eave, the bus-stop name blob.

Line grades follow plan §Rules 7: ruler lines 0.5–0.9 px alpha 115–195 (`rline`, hand-spaced parallels ±5 %, overshoot 0.2–2 px); 起稿 and 田垄 through `bline` 0.38–0.55 px alpha 50–90; no fills of silk colour anywhere — the wall face is blank because nothing is drawn on it.

## Randomness

All `rr/ri/R/noise` calls happen inside `build()`; closures capture arrays of finished numbers (`ds`, `dots`, `segs` inside the ctx primitives). `footprint()` consumes no randomness: steps `n` is resolved in `build()`, and geometry helpers (`quayGeom`, `wallGeom`, `pierGeom`, `busstopGeom`) are deterministic in the spec, so `footprint` and `build` see the same shapes.

## Round 3 (D-08) · what changed and what the render shows

The B2 tell for this module: 「the foreground trees stand like pins below a diagonal boundary. The intervening ground has little shape or bearing capacity」 and 「ochre gathers in a succession of soft patches below the bank」. The answer, in craft terms: each strip's front edge is now one continuous line that dips and rises like a real bank, the strip carries a few short contour lines and 点苔 that make it a surface, its earth is one graded 罩染 that is full at the edge and gone before the back with one or two large reserves left blank, the bank's 皴 run down the slope to the water in loose groups instead of an even row of stubs, the stone courses of a quay or 防洪墙 are written only near the top and dissolve below, and every tree on a terrace stands in a small mound of its own (`GROUND.foot`) with 皴 on it and a shadow dot at the trunk. Line grades follow `ctx.INK`: edge lines structural, 皴 / contours / mound 皴 texture (no head or bead — core does that), only the 缆桩 and the mound arcs a shade heavier.

Render (`review/module-ground.png`, three trees on the bank with `foot()`, CW=0; the canvas sits at page (51,75) in this screenshot): at 1× the street → 防洪墙 → quay → water and street → bank → water sequences read as graded bands of earth with a shaped front edge; the trees stand in visible mounds rather than on the line; the bank shows slanted 皴 groups and contour dashes; the wall's courses now stop under the coping. First pass had the bank 皴 as long straight strokes in both directions (read as rain) — regrouped, one lean per strip, shorter, ending on the edge; the first mound was too small to read at 1× and its 皴 ran past the ground line like grass — mound widened, arcs raised to structural, 皴 confined to the mound. Left as is: the street's ochre reads as a band along the kerb because it fades to nothing at 60 % depth — that is the D-08 instruction; the built edges wave by ~1 px, less than the bank, so the kerb stays a kerb.

**main.js must**: call `GROUND.foot(ctx,{x,y,w,z})` under every tree standing on a terrace (x,y = the trunk's ground contact from the tree module, w ≈ its base width, z = the tree's z); read `slots.edge` if it needs the wavy edge (e.g. to seat a figure exactly on a bank); nothing else changed in the contract — `terrace`, `steps`, `bankLine`, zone kinds and slots keep their names and shapes. The default terrace `ink` is 0.24 now; the 0.2–0.28 values main.js passes still work.

## Notes for the integrator

- Walls of any length are one zone now; stamp `fp.slices||[fp]` (see Public functions). The old advice to cut receding walls into 48-px pieces is withdrawn — the pieces were the reviewer's 「five short pieces」.
- Every street/quay/bank in main.js should be a `GROUND.terrace` chain: for each level, the front edge polyline right→left and a depth up to the next level's edge; call back-to-front after the footprints are stamped (terraces stamp nothing). The bank's `bankLine` is called last with the joined water-edge polyline.
- `pier`: `rise` is ignored — the deck is horizontal; place the pier's `(x,y)` a few px **in front of** the shore line and take sitting figures' `y` from `slots.seats`.
- The quay face's `z` is the platform edge; the water polygon should start at `slots.water` (the face bottom), and `CROWD` may place feet on `slots.steps` (their x-range is not `solidAt`).
- The pier's `slots.seats` carry `z` = the pier's z; pass it to the sitting figures so their hanging legs draw over the deck face.
- `crossing`, `treepit`, `busstop`, `railing` also work as standalone zones with the same fields.
