Out of Line

A line-painting machine encounters a paper flower — and finds a new way to draw.

一辆只会画直线的小车，遇见了一朵纸花。

Made through code / 用代码制作

The scenes, animation and cameras were authored as Blender programs; the score and sound effects were composed and synthesized in code. No diffusion, image, video or music generation models were used.

场景、动画和镜头由 agent 编写 Blender 程序完成，配乐与音效通过代码创作和合成。全片未调用扩散模型或图像、视频、音乐生成模型。

This first film informed the general ALIGN-video skills: study a reference, build and render the work, review what the audience receives, then revise the program.

这是 ALIGN-video 的首个案例。影片的制作经验被整理成通用技能：研究参考、编程制作、看成片、审阅，再把反馈落实到下一版。

Production and review / 制作与评审

Claude led the original production with Codex reviews of asset stills, story structure and film image sequences. Codex completed v3 with self-review. The final review used stills and action sequences; it was not a new independent audiovisual review at playback speed. The skills were distilled from this work afterward.

前期由 Claude 制作、Codex 审阅资产画面、故事和影片抽帧。v3 由 Codex 接手续作并自审，检查基于静帧和动作序列，未进行新一轮正常速度、有声整片的独立审阅。技能正文是在这次实践之后整理的。

Existing craft reference: Kling’s THE BEAT. Out of Line’s story, characters and shots are original. Environment and concrete texture assets came from Poly Haven under CC0. See sources and credits.
